Plain-language summary

One Square is a casual game. We do not create accounts and we do not collect your name, email address, contacts, photos, precise location, microphone, camera, or health data. Your game progress and settings are stored locally on your device. The game shows advertisements through Unity LevelPlay, and our advertising partners may collect your advertising ID and limited device information to deliver and measure ads, prevent fraud, and comply with law. You can change your personalized or non-personalized ad choice in the in-game menu (top-right) or limit ad personalization in your device settings at any time.

1. Who we are

This Privacy Policy explains how fuduoduoliao ("we", "us", or "our") handles information in connection with the mobile game One Square (the "Game"), distributed on Google Play for Android. An iOS version may be released later, and this Privacy Policy will apply to it when available.

For privacy questions or requests, contact us at fuduoduoliao@qq.com.

2. Information stored on your device

The Game stores the following information locally on your device. It is not uploaded to us:

  • Game progress, unlocked content, scores, and in-game currency balances.
  • Settings such as language, music and sound volume, and accessibility preferences.
  • Advertising consent status and ad frequency counters used to respect your choices.

Local data remains on your device until you clear the Game's data or uninstall the Game.

5. How we and our partners use information

  • To display rewarded, interstitial, and banner advertisements.
  • To measure ad performance, cap frequency, and prevent invalid traffic and fraud.
  • To comply with legal obligations and enforce our terms.
  • To maintain the security, stability, and performance of the Game.

Where required by law, we rely on your consent (for example, for personalized advertising and access to advertising identifiers). Otherwise, we rely on legitimate interests such as delivering non-personalized ads, measuring aggregate performance, and preventing fraud.

6. Your choices and rights

Android

You can reset or delete your advertising ID and opt out of personalized ads in Settings > Google > Ads (the path may vary by device).

iOS

You can limit ad tracking in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking, and reset the advertising identifier in Settings > Privacy & Security > Apple Advertising. If an iOS version is released and requests App Tracking Transparency permission, you may deny it and continue to use the Game.

All users

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to the processing of your personal data, and to withdraw consent. To exercise these rights, contact us at fuduoduoliao@qq.com. You may also lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

California residents: we do not sell personal information for monetary consideration. Certain advertising activities may be considered "sharing" or "targeted advertising" under the CCPA/CPRA. You can opt out using the device controls described above or by contacting us.

In-game advertising choice: The Game asks you to choose between personalized and non-personalized advertising on first launch, and you can change that choice at any time from the in-game menu (top-right) under "Privacy & Ads". We do not use a certified consent management platform, and the Game is not offered for distribution in the EEA, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland.

7. Children's privacy

The Game is not directed to children under 13 (or under 16 in the European Economic Area, where applicable). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will take appropriate steps to delete it.

8. Data retention

We retain local game data on your device until you clear it or uninstall the Game. Advertising partners retain data according to their own policies and applicable law. We do not maintain a server-side player profile for the Game.

9. Security and international transfers

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information. Advertising providers may process data in countries other than your own; they are responsible for implementing appropriate safeguards for international transfers under their policies and applicable law.

10. Permissions

The Android build requests only the permissions needed for network access and advertising: android.permission.INTERNET, android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE, and com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID.
