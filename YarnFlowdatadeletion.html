How to delete your data in one minute

1. Local game data (progress, coins, bobbins, saved artwork, settings): open Settings > Apps > Yarn Flow > Storage & cache > Clear storage, or simply uninstall the game. It is deleted immediately and cannot be restored.

2. Advertising data (advertising ID and ad interaction data held by our advertising partners): email fuduoduoliao@qq.com with the subject Data deletion request - Yarn Flow. We respond within 30 days.

3. You can also reset your advertising ID yourself at any time (see section 4), which stops any future association with your device.

1. What we hold

Yarn Flow does not have accounts, sign-in, or a server-side player profile. We do not store your game progress, artwork, or in-game currency on any server: they exist only on your device. The only data connected to you that lives outside your device is advertising data collected by third-party advertising and mediation partners (Unity LevelPlay / ironSource, Unity Ads, AppLovin and Google AdMob) when ads are requested or shown, as described in our Privacy Policy.

2. Delete local game data (no request needed)

All progress, unlocked bobbins, coins, saved works and settings are stored in the app's private storage on your device. You can delete them yourself at any time:

  1. Open the device Settings.
  2. Tap Apps (or Apps & notifications) > Yarn Flow.
  3. Tap Storage & cache > Clear storage (or Clear data).
  4. Alternatively, uninstall the game; removing the app removes all of its local data.

This action is immediate and irreversible. Cleared or uninstalled progress cannot be restored by us, because we never had a copy of it.

3. Request deletion of advertising data

If you want the advertising data associated with your device deleted, send an email to fuduoduoliao@qq.com and include:

  • Subject line: Data deletion request - Yarn Flow
  • Your Google Advertising ID (Android: Settings > Google > Ads > Advertising ID; you can copy it there). Please do not send any other personal information.
  • The approximate date you last played the game (optional, helps us locate request logs).

Within 30 days we will: (a) confirm what, if anything, we hold about you — normally nothing except the email correspondence itself; (b) forward your request to the advertising partners listed above and ask them to delete the associated advertising data; and (c) reply to you with the outcome. If we need to verify that the request comes from the device owner, we may ask you to confirm the advertising ID from the same device.

4. Self-service: reset your advertising ID

Resetting the advertising ID disconnects your device from any data previously collected under the old identifier and starts a fresh one, without waiting for a request:

  • Android: Settings > Google > Ads > Delete advertising ID (or Reset advertising ID).
  • In the game: open the in-game menu (top-right) > Privacy & Ads to switch between personalized and non-personalized advertising.

6. Children

The game is not directed to children under 13. If you believe a child has used the game and you want the associated advertising data deleted, send a request using the steps in section 3 and we will act on it promptly.
